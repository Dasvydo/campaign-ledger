## What the numbers suggest

- **Denmark is the weakest market so far.** 8 touches produced 2 replies (0 positive), 2 leads and 0 held calls. Lithuania on 9 touches produced 2 held calls and EUR 3,197.00 MRR.
  Both sides of that comparison are under 30 touches, so it is a hint, not a result. Do not cut a market on it yet.
- **Denmark has 1 booked call(s) and none held.** Check whether those dates have passed before treating it as a no-show problem.
- **US / global has 3 booked call(s) and none held.** Check whether those dates have passed before treating it as a no-show problem.
- **Meta ads costs EUR 89.28 per booked call** at EUR 178.55 spent. Against $89 per seat per month, one 10 seat firm covers that in the first week of a paid month.
- **Reels brought 2 leads and no booked calls.**
- **Meta ads is pulling the wrong size of firm.** 2 of 4 leads are 1-9 seats.
- **HyperFrames is the strongest lane on engagement** at 5.11% across 3 placement(s), against Static ad at 0.52%.
- 1 localised item(s) are under 2% engagement. Worth checking the dub quality before concluding the market does not want the content - both languages ship marked NEEDS NATIVE CHECK.
